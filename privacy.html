RoadIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information the RoadIQ app ("RoadIQ", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. RoadIQ is published by Usman Bukhari, who is the data controller. Contact: usmanbukhari541@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details – your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider) when you create an account.
  • Profile – the display name and avatar you choose, your role (learner or instructor), your planned test date and daily goal.
  • Learning progress – quiz sessions, the answers you give, scores, time taken, streaks, daily challenges, XP, coins and gems. This lets us sync your progress across devices.
  • Leaderboard – your display name, avatar and weekly XP are shown to other RoadIQ users on the weekly leaderboard.
  • Instructor connections – if you choose to link with a driving instructor using a connection code, that instructor can see your progress. You can revoke the link at any time.
  • Purchases – if you buy RoadIQ Pro, Google Play processes the payment. We receive purchase status (not your card details) through our purchase provider, RevenueCat, using an anonymous app user ID, to unlock Pro features.

RoadIQ does not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and does not show ads.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving and syncing your progress, leaderboards and instructor features.
  • To unlock and restore Pro purchases.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are performance of a contract (providing the app you signed up for) and our legitimate interests in running and securing the service.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – account authentication and database hosting.
  • RevenueCat – purchase management.
  • Google Play – app distribution and payments.

Other users see only your leaderboard details, and an instructor you link with sees your progress. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage, security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected with access controls so each user can only access their own data. Some progress is also stored on your device so the app works offline. We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and synced progress are permanently deleted from our database.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account – in the app go to Account → Delete Account, or see how to request deletion.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct or export your data by emailing us.
  • If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

RoadIQ is intended for people preparing for the driving theory test and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
