Delete your RoadIQ account

You can permanently delete your RoadIQ account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open RoadIQ and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Without the app

Email usmanbukhari541@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "RoadIQ account deletion". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email and password), profile, learning progress, streaks, rewards, leaderboard entries, instructor connections and connection codes are permanently deleted. We do not keep any of this data after deletion. Purchase records held by Google Play, the Apple App Store and RevenueCat are kept by those providers as required for financial and legal reasons.
